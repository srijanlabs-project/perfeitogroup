What We Offer

Core IT Solutions for Manufacturing

End-to-end technology solutions engineered for the unique demands of modern manufacturing environments — from greenfield plants to complex multi-site operations.

Data Centre Infrastructure
Tier III/IV data center design, server rooms, UPS, precision cooling, and structured cabling engineered for demanding manufacturing facilities.
Networking Solutions
LAN/WAN design, WiFi 6 enterprise networking, MPLS links, and SD-WAN for seamless multi-plant connectivity across your entire manufacturing network.
Infrastructure Build
End-to-end IT infrastructure build including racking, cabling, and commissioning for both greenfield and brownfield manufacturing plants.
End-User Computing
Laptops, desktops, workstations, thin clients, printers and peripherals. Complete lifecycle management and hardware refresh programs for your workforce.
Enterprise Servers
HPE, Dell PowerEdge, and Lenovo servers optimized for ERP, MES, and SCADA applications. Available in rack and tower configurations to suit your plant layout.
Video Conferencing
Polycom, Logitech, and Cisco meeting room solutions enabling cross-plant collaboration and management reviews with crystal-clear audio and video quality.
Facility Management Services (FMS)
Ongoing IT infrastructure management, AMC, helpdesk, asset tracking, and SLA-based support to keep your manufacturing operations running without interruption.

How We Work

Our Proven Deployment Process

A structured, milestone-driven approach that minimises disruption to your production environment at every stage.

01
Site Survey & Assessment
Comprehensive audit of existing IT infrastructure, power, cooling, cabling and connectivity across your plant or campus.
02
Architecture Design
Detailed network topology, server specifications, and data centre design tailored to your production loads and future growth.
03
Phased Deployment
Scheduled rollout in production-safe windows. Zero-downtime cabling, racking, configuration, and commissioning by certified engineers.
04
Ongoing FMS Support
Post-deployment managed services: 24/7 helpdesk, proactive monitoring, AMC, asset lifecycle management, and SLA-bound response.
