Our Solutions

Complete Infrastructure Design & Build Capabilities

From initial network planning to final commissioning, we deliver every component of your infrastructure under one roof — backed by OEM-certified engineers.

Network Infrastructure Design

End-to-end LAN/WAN network architecture design, including topology planning, bandwidth analysis, redundancy design, and documentation. We deliver enterprise-grade networks built on Cisco, HP Aruba, and Juniper platforms.

Cisco · HP Aruba · Juniper

Server Room & Data Centre Build

Turnkey data centre and server room construction — rack planning, cable management, cooling systems, raised flooring, fire suppression and environmental monitoring. Compliant with TIA-942 standards.

TIA-942 · Rack & Stack · Cooling

Structured Cabling

Cat6/Cat6A/Fibre optic cabling infrastructure meeting ISO/IEC 11801 and TIA-568 standards. Includes cable trays, patch panels, face plates and complete testing with certification reports from Fluke networks.

Cat6A · Fibre · Fluke Certified

Power & UPS Systems

Design and installation of reliable power infrastructure including UPS systems, PDUs, isolation transformers, and earthing. We work with APC, Eaton, and Emerson for critical power protection in IT environments.

APC · Eaton · Emerson

IT Hardware Procurement

Authorised procurement and supply of servers, networking equipment, storage systems, and end-user computing devices. We source from top OEMs including Dell, HPE, Lenovo, and Cisco at competitive enterprise pricing.

Dell · HPE · Lenovo · Cisco

Project Management & Commissioning

Dedicated project management from scope definition through to final handover. Includes site coordination, vendor management, milestone tracking, acceptance testing, and complete as-built documentation.

Our Process

How We Deliver Your Infrastructure

A structured, transparent four-phase approach that ensures every project is delivered on schedule, within budget, and to specification.

1

Site Assessment

Our engineers conduct a thorough on-site audit of existing infrastructure, capacity requirements, environmental constraints, and regulatory considerations to establish a solid project baseline.

2

System Architecture

We produce detailed network diagrams, rack layouts, cabling schematics and a full bill of materials. All designs are reviewed and signed off before any procurement begins.

3

Installation & Testing

Certified technicians execute the installation following manufacturer guidelines. All components are rigorously tested, with results documented against agreed acceptance criteria.

4

Handover & Documentation

Formal handover with complete as-built drawings, configuration backups, warranty registrations, and a knowledge transfer session. Optional AMC support packages available post-handover.
