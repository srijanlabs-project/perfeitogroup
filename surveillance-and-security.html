Our Solutions

End-to-End Physical & Cyber Security Solutions

From IP cameras to network firewalls, we design and deploy layered security architectures that protect every dimension of your organisation.

IP CCTV Systems

Full-HD and 4K IP camera deployments using Hikvision, Dahua, and Axis platforms — covering indoor, outdoor, PTZ, and covert applications. Paired with NVR/DVR setup and a centralised Video Management System (VMS) for seamless multi-site viewing and recording.

Hikvision · Dahua · Axis · VMS

Access Control Systems

Multi-factor access control solutions including biometric readers (fingerprint & face), RFID card-based systems, PIN keypads, and boom barriers. Integrated with visitor management software for complete entry audit trails across all entry points.

Biometric · RFID · HID · Boom Barriers

AI Video Analytics

Intelligent video analytics overlaid on your existing or new camera infrastructure — including motion detection, facial recognition, crowd density analysis, perimeter intrusion alerts, and loitering detection. Real-time alerts delivered to your security team instantly.

Face Recognition · Crowd Detection · Perimeter

Network Security

Comprehensive cybersecurity services including next-generation firewall deployment, endpoint protection, VAPT (Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing), and network segmentation. We help organisations align to ISO 27001 and CERT-In guidelines.

NGFW · VAPT · Endpoint · ISO 27001

Alarm & Intrusion Detection

Professionally designed and installed intruder alarm systems featuring PIR sensors, door/window contacts, glass-break detectors, and panic buttons — all integrated with a central monitoring station for immediate response. Suitable for offices, warehouses, and retail premises.

PIR · Door Contacts · Panic Alarm · Monitoring

Central Monitoring Room Setup

Complete design and build of Security Operations Centres (SOC) and Central Monitoring Rooms — including video wall systems, operator workstations, KVM switches, UPS backup, and network infrastructure for 24/7 surveillance operations.

Video Wall · SOC · 24/7 Operations
Our Process

A Systematic Approach to Site Security

We follow a proven four-phase methodology that ensures your security system is correctly sized, expertly installed, and actively maintained.

1

Security Audit

Our security consultants conduct a thorough on-site risk assessment — identifying vulnerabilities, blind spots, entry point risks, and compliance requirements to build a complete threat picture before any design work begins.

2

System Design

We produce detailed security system drawings including camera placement plans, access control zone maps, cabling schematics, and a full equipment bill of materials — reviewed and agreed before procurement.

3

Installation & Integration

Certified installation engineers deploy all hardware and software, integrate subsystems (CCTV, access control, alarms), and conduct thorough commissioning and user acceptance testing before handover.

4

Monitoring & AMC

Optional 24/7 remote monitoring via our NOC, supported by Annual Maintenance Contracts covering preventive maintenance visits, software updates, and priority response to faults and incidents.
